## Night lighting cues (from state-farm.jpg, the one night photo)

- Warm sodium/LED spill `#FFB86A` from concourses, entrances and under
  overhangs is what makes the campus read as inhabited. Put it under the State
  Farm rim, in Foellinger's portico, under the Union portico, at Beckman's arch.
- Street lamps are warm globes on dark posts, spaced along the Quad walks
  (see foellinger.jpg — two globe lamps flank the portico stairs).
- Trees on the Quad are broad elms; the canopy is dense and nearly black at
  night with the underside lit by the lamps.

## Campus ground

- Quad lawn daylight `#4E8A3F`; night: deep green `#14301E` with lamp pools.
- Walks are pale concrete `#C8C2B4`, so at night they read lighter than lawn.
- Roads: dark asphalt `#2B2D33`, yellow centre lines on major roads.
