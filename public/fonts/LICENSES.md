## Refreshing

Fetch `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Silkscreen:wght@400;700&family=Jersey+10&family=Pixelify+Sans:wght@400;600;700&family=VT323&display=swap` with a current Chrome `User-Agent` (older UAs are served woff/ttf), download each `url(...)` and keep the `unicode-range` lines in the `@font-face` block at the top of `public/styles.css` in sync.
